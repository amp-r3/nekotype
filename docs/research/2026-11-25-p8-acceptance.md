## The export against the code

Each screen was compared with its description in the export's [README](../design/source/README.md) and its strings in `shared/i18n`:

- **The home** (decisions 140–145, 153–155): two bands from 640 px, the order by width, the path as a `Segmented` chain with the caret, the sleeping cat with `none`; built and checked in PR 7 and M4.
- **Progress** (130, 137, 146): the title with `track shown`, the chart, the blocks; the answer-time chart follows the switch (`no katakana answers yet`).
- **The kana details dialog** (147): the rows `state`, `memory` (`holds less than a day`, `mastered from 21 days`), `recall`, `mixed up with`, `recognition`.
- **The session** (149): the ticks under the session line, `miss` on a task answered with an error.
- **The summary** (148): the forecast change in medians, the kana as cells, `continue` and `home`.
- **The navigation** (150): `learn · progress · settings`, the open link underlined.

The states of P9–P12 are in the previews under `later` (decision 152); none is in the code: no task type, setting, string or screen of a later phase exists, and the session's audio provider stays silent until P10.

The component guidelines of the export still name `/stats` where they mean `/progress` (`ActivityMap`, `Notice`, `ResultChart`, `Stat`, `Tooltip`, the usage notes of some tokens), and `Button` names `to the table` as its example of `secondary`. They describe no screen, and `docs/design/source/` is never edited by hand: the next design session corrects them in the design-system artifact.
