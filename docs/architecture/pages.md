# Pages and shared UI

Part of the [architecture description](README.md) of the current code.

## The learning screen (`pages/learn`)

- `pages/learn` is the learning screen: the chart of the selected track (`KanaChart`: blocks side by side with captions, a legend; an introduced cell calls `onOpen`, which opens the kana details dialog of `features/kana-details` — the chart draws no panel), the progress line on one row with the track switch — `mastered X of N`, the streak (hidden until the first answer; a first-visit line takes its place) and `due: n` (hidden at 0) — `continue` (focused when the screen is ready, the only action), then one reserved `Notice` line (loading, a failed read with `retry`, or progress that cannot be saved). It reads `learnView`, the fold of the log (`entities/learn`); `n` counts the kana of both tracks whose `dueFrom` has passed (`dueCount`), exactly the due marks of the chart plus those of the other track.
  - `model/session.ts` is `createLearnSession(deps)` (`Clock`, `Scheduler`, `Rng`, the learn store, `AudioProvider`), a session of 30 tasks on the planner of `@nekotype/model` (ADR 104). A task is `nextTask(modelState(), { now, track, previous, rng })`, called when the task is shown (from `start()`, the hold timer, Enter, Space or a click), never from a key handler. The handler of a task finishes its view and queues the record with `scheduler.setTimeout(…, 0)`; the callback calls `introduce` or `recordAnswer`, so a key does no fold, grade or IndexedDB call. `start()` writes the `start` event, the end of the session writes `end` and asks for a sync; leaving early writes no `end`.
  - The kana that a wrong recall matched (`lib/wrong-kana.ts`): the accepted prefix plus the first rejected key, when it is one spelling of one kana of the task's track in the strictness. Recognition options take the first distractor from the kana's confusions (`lib/distractors.ts`).
  - The romaji shown to the learner is `displayRomaji` (`lib/romaji`): the table's canonical spelling, except ん and ン, shown as `n`; an introduction takes only the romaji it shows (another spelling is a rejected key), recall takes every spelling of the learning strictness.
  - After a wrong choice the screen draws the hint to go on: `enter` and `next`, or a `next` button under `pointer: coarse`.
  - `lib` holds the distractors, the summary (introduced, learned, faster, slower, confused, from two `LearnView`s) and the hook binding the session to the input source.
  - The session runtime and the summary panel are lazy chunks; the summary screen (its actions and the place of the panel) is part of the session runtime chunk, loaded before a session can end (ADR 067). The model is a chunk of its own (`vite.config.ts` names it `model`; `check-budget.mjs` keeps it out of the initial chunk and out of every route's static imports), loaded by the learn store and shared with the session runtime.
  - ADR 015 keeps the session in the page, which is its only consumer.
- `shared/ui` holds `KanaTask`, `createTaskController`, `OptionCard` and `KanaChart` as presentational components: data, states and strings come as props, and they import nothing from `entities` or `pages`. The input source (`shared/lib/input`) serves the learning session.

The kana table and the binding of `packages/engine` to it live in `pages/learn/lib/prompt` ([ADR 096](../adr/096-learning-only-data-and-sync-format-2.md)): the table (`data`: `basicRows`, `dakutenRows`, `yoonRows`, `extendedRows`, `allKanaRows`), the binding (`engine.ts`: `compile`, `start`, `step`, `isComplete`, `spellingsFor`, `canonical` and the rest of the matcher API) and `canonicalFor` (`canonical.ts`). A recall task and an introduction compile their kana with it. The generated word and phrase chunks stay in `entities/prompt/content/`: nothing in `apps/web/src` imports them and `check-budget.mjs` fails a build whose manifest holds one (no content is shipped before P12, ADR 093).

## The progress screen (`pages/progress`)

- `pages/progress` (a lazy route at `/progress`, P8; before it `pages/stats` at `/stats`, which is redirected here; ADR 076, ADR 111, design decisions 130, 137 and 146) is the learning progress, all of the screen: the kana table of the shown track, then the statistics; no section switch, no URL hash. It reads `learnView` of the learn store (the fold of the log: `LearnView.mastered` and `LogStats`, P7), shapes the data in `lib/` (pure functions with tests) and draws it in `ui/`.
  - **Page** (`ui/progress-page.tsx`): the `h1` with the track switch on its right, then the status of the learn store. The switch is the page's local state (`useState`, starting at `settings.learn.track`, never written back): it decides the track of the table and of the answer-time chart. Once the store is ready: the table (`ui/progress-chart.tsx`: `KanaChart` with `lib/chart.ts` — a copy of the `/` chart's strings and rows without the details panel, which `/` drops when P8 PR 4 takes the chart off it — and `onOpen` calling `openKanaDetails`), then the statistics. Loading shows `loading…`, an error the message with `retry` (`retryLearn`); with no answer and no started session the table stays (every kana locked) and one `EmptyState` with an action to `/` stands under it instead of the blocks. Only the page reads the clock (`defaultClock`); the section gets `today` as a prop.
  - **Learning** (`ui/learning-section.tsx`): a grid of four equal columns, two below 640 px. `streak and activity`: column 1 is the current streak in the large mono face, the best streak (`streaks` of `entities/learn`, from the keys of `LogStats.days`) and the active days of the last 30 (`activeDaysIn`, a fixed window), columns 2–4 the activity map. Then four `Stat`s: mastered kana of both tracks, sessions started, sessions finished. Then `answer time` across the page for the shown track (its caption starts with the track's name; a track without recall answers shows `no {track} answers yet` instead of the chart and the period switch); the period `Segmented` is 7 / 30 days or all, 30 by default. `lib/answer-time.ts`: `dayBars` (one bar per day from the exact times of `DayLog.recall` through `quartilesOf`: the ⌈n/4⌉-th and ⌈3n/4⌉-th shortest time and the exact median; one bar per Monday-to-Sunday week above 45 days on the axis; no day is dropped), `msAxis` (covers the bars and the target answer time, which is a dashed `reference` line). A period without answers shows a notice with `show all time`.
  - **Chart** (`ui/stats-chart.tsx`): draws `ranges` (a bar from the first to the third quartile of a day or a week with a tick at its median) at the container's real width. Axes: `axisOf` in `lib/trend.ts` picks the smallest step of a list for which the snapped domain has at most four intervals, so every axis ends on a tick; `timeAxis` follows the period. Hover picks the nearest bar; ← → `Home` `End` `Esc` walk them; the tooltip lines are built only for the bar under the cursor, and the tooltip is the chart's `aria-describedby`.
  - **Activity map** (`ui/activity-map.tsx`): a column per Monday-first week and a button per day up to today. The map measures its own width (`ResizeObserver`) and `fitMap` in `lib/activity.ts` gives the frame: as many weeks as fit at 16 px, at most 26, cells growing to 28 px (not rounded, so the last column reaches the edge); without a width it draws 12 weeks. `activityDays` joins the recall answers of both tracks per day, marks every day with an answer of any task as active and the days of the current streak (while it ends today or yesterday: the cells with a dot, `is-run`); `levelOf` gives the fill step 0–5 at 1 / 10 / 25 / 50 / 100 answers (an active day without recall answers is 1). One Tab stop (today until a cell is chosen) with the arrows by day and week, `Home` / `End`; focus or hover shows the tooltip (the date, the answers, `current streak`, `today`).
  - **Styles and shared parts**: `ui/period-switch.tsx` (`PeriodSwitch`) and `ui/track-switch.tsx` (`TrackSwitch`, labelled `track shown`); `lib/days.ts` holds `DAY_MS`, `HALF_DAY_MS`, `MIN_DAYS_ALL`, `mondayOf` and the `Period` type. The screen's styles (`.nk-rc*`, `.nk-am*`) are in the lazy `ui/stats.css`, not in the initial stylesheet. `Stat`, `Cat` and `EmptyState` are in `shared/ui` and out of its barrel, so they stay in the lazy chunk.
  - **Tests**: `lib/*.test.ts` cover the pure functions; `ui/*.test.tsx` are per widget and page (`progress-page`, `progress-page-error`, `learning-section`, `learning-section-data`, `stats-chart`, `activity-map`); `e2e/progress.spec.ts` seeds a log and checks the map on a phone and on a desktop, the streak, totals and chart, and the table: a cell opens the details by mouse, `Enter` and `Space`, `Esc` and `✕` give the focus back, and the switch leaves the learning track alone.

## The kana details (`features/kana-details`)

- The dialog of design decision 147 (P8, ADR 111), a feature slice because `pages/learn` and `pages/progress` both open it and pages do not import each other. `index.ts` exports `KanaDetailsHost`, `openKanaDetails(key)`, `closeKanaDetails`, `openKanaKey` (a signal) and `prefetchKanaDetails`.
  - `model/open.ts`: the open key as a signal. `openKanaDetails` does nothing while `immersive` is on, so a session never opens it.
  - `ui/kana-details-host.tsx` (in the initial chunk, rendered once by `App.tsx`): loads `ui/kana-details-dialog.tsx` and the dictionary part `details` through `lib/loader.ts` when the first key opens (they are also prefetched on idle) and draws nothing until then. Once loaded the dialog stays mounted and only opens and closes (`open={key !== null}`), so a close by `✕` or the backdrop returns the focus to the opener like `Esc` does.
  - `ui/kana-details-dialog.tsx` (lazy): the shared `Dialog` (its title takes nodes: the kana in `jp-test-sm` and its romaji) with a `dl` of the rows from `lib/lines.ts` (`detailsRows(KanaView, now)`: state, memory with its 2 px stroke, recall, mixups, recognition). It reads `learnView.kana`, so a locked kana has no details.
  - `check-budget.mjs` fails when the dialog's chunk (`DETAILS_ENTRY`) is missing from the manifest, in the initial chunk or a static import of a route chunk.

## The about screen (`pages/about`)

- `pages/about` (a lazy route; ADR 066) shows the terms of Nekotype (`ui/terms-section.tsx`: code, name and cat, fonts) and nothing else: no sources, no phrase authors, no lazy chunk beyond the route, because no content is shipped before P12 (ADR 093).
  - The kana audio (P10) and stored data (P14) sections are not rendered yet.

## The settings screen (`pages/settings`)

- `pages/settings` (a lazy route) is in sections: `Learning` (the learning strictness as a `Segmented`, the target answer time as a `Select` of nine values from 1000 to 3000 ms in steps of 250) and `Data` (reset progress, with a confirmation `Dialog`); each row has the setting's name and what it does on the left and the control on the right, under the text below 640 px. `Select` is in `shared/ui` and out of its barrel.

## SVG in components

- SVG attributes are written in kebab-case (`stroke-width`, `text-anchor`): Preact core writes JSX names as they are, and the browser ignores a camelCase one. `app/svg-attributes.test.ts` fails on a camelCase attribute.
