# Entities: stores and their contracts

Part of the [architecture description](README.md) of the current code.

## `entities/settings`

- Holds the learning settings (`learnStrictness`, `learnTrack`, `learnTargetMs`; the sound flag is stored only) as `ReadonlySignal`s, and the actions that change them (`setLearnStrictness`, `setTrack`, `setTargetMs`). `Settings` is `{ learn, updatedAt }` ([ADR 096](../adr/096-learning-only-data-and-sync-format-2.md)).
  - `initSettings(db)` reads the `settings` record (`current`) from IndexedDB, replaces a value that fails `isSettings` with the defaults and sets `settingsReady`; `app/route-view.tsx` shows the loading line until then.
  - Each action writes the whole `Settings` document at once (no debounce, nothing waits for `pagehide`) and sets `updatedAt` from the `Clock`, then posts on the `BroadcastChannel` `nekotype:settings` (another tab reads the document again); a failed write calls `reportWriteFailure()`.
  - `LearnTrack` derives from `ValidSettings`, so the slice imports no other.
- A saved value that fails validation (a document with a `typing` block of P5, for example) is replaced by the defaults, with no conversion (ADR 061).

## `entities/learn`

- **The screens read the log store (P7, [ADR 103](../adr/103-log-on-the-client.md), [ADR 104](../adr/104-learning-on-the-model.md)).** `model/log.ts` keeps the learning log in the IndexedDB store `log` (schema 6): `appendEvents` gives events their `n` and `at` inside one transaction, `readLog` reads and de-duplicates the pages, `readUnsent` finds the own events after `sentN` (the sync engine reads them through the index), `clearLog` empties the log and the forecast history. An event time is an integer: the clock's fraction is rounded when the event is made. `model/learn-store.ts` loads the log and the `@nekotype/model` chunk together, folds the log, applies each action to the fold at once and exposes `learnView` (`describe` of the fold, `null` until ready), `modelState()` for the planner, `learnStatus`, `learnReady`, `initLearn(targetMs)`, `retryLearn`, `startSession`, `endSession`, `introduce`, `recordAnswer`, `resetLearning`, `reloadLearning` and `flushLearning`. It writes later through the `Scheduler`, once for a burst, and folds again on an event out of order, on a pull, on a changed target and when another tab took the same `n`. No action touches IndexedDB, so a keystroke handler never does.
  - **Insights (P8, [ADR 112](../adr/112-insights-on-the-main-thread.md)).** The store also exposes `learnVersion` (grows at each publish of `learnView`), `startSnapshot()` (the state just before the last `start`, folded together with the log: the prefix, a copy, then the rest), `loadedModel()` and `loadedInsights()`, and tells `onSessionStart` and `onLearnReset` listeners. `model/insights-state.ts` (initial chunk) holds the signal `insights` (`outline`, `stronger`, `forecast`), `requestInsights(track)` and `reasonAt(now, track)`: a request schedules step 1 (what got stronger, the outline) and step 2 (the history, the forecast) in the injected `Scheduler`, is dropped when `learnVersion` or the track moves on, and `startSession` drops it. `model/insights.ts` (a lazy chunk, loaded with the model) holds `computeStepOne` and `createInsightsEngine`; `model/forecasts.ts` its history, the store `forecasts` (read once, written through, dropped when the log is emptied). `model/insights-types.ts` has the types only. No screen calls `requestInsights` before P8 PR 4.
  - `lib/rows.ts` (initial chunk): `chartRows` (the table's rows with the gaps of や ゆ よ) and `headOfRow(rowId)` (the row's head as the chart shows it: `k`, `sh`, `''` for the vowels). `lib/path.ts`: `pathOf(view, track)`, the blocks and rows of a track with their states `done`, `current` (the first row not done, the row `nextRow` of the model forecasts) and `locked`, and the counts of introduced and learned kana; a lazy chunk, with the home's panels. `lib/chart.ts`: `cellInfos(track, view, now)`, the cell of every kana of a track from `LearnView` (state `locked`, `learning`, `learned` or `mastered`, due, next review, median, accuracy, confusions, recognitions). `lib/progress.ts`: `dueCount(view, now)` and `streakOfDays(days, today)`. `lib/tracks.ts`: track composition and dependencies; `lib/answer-time.ts`: recall answer time with K.
  - `lib/test-view.ts` builds a `LearnView` from events for tests, with the real model.
- `packages/engine` also holds the table of visually similar kana and the track composition helpers (`kanaOf`, `kanaRows`) used for distractors and the chart.
