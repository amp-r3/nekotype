# ADR 076: The `/stats` Screen: One Section at a Time, Filters, Test Marks and Heat Maps

- **Status:** In force. Amends [ADR 058](058-learning-first.md) (it took heat maps out of the MVP and gave `/stats` one line per chart) and [ADR 059](059-design-system-v10.md) (design decisions 68 and 78). Replaces plan decision T11 (one KPM line over the last 100 tests of every mode).
- **Context:** After P4 the `/stats` build matched the design, and the design read badly (design audit, G1–G3): Learning and Typing ran together in one long column of small headings; the hiragana / katakana switch looked like a page filter while it changed one chart, and stood over an empty section for a user without answers; one test was a dot at the edge of an empty chart with a fixed 0–120 axis, two tests one line across the page; every mode shared one KPM line, though speeds of different modes do not compare. The maintainer also asked for a chart that is not a standard line, for filters and more metrics, and for a heat map (F2). The design session of 13 November 2026 settled these as design decisions 100–112.
- **Decision:**
  1. **One section at a time.** Under the screen title a switch selects `learning` or `typing`; one section is on the screen. The section with data opens first, learning when both have data. The Counters section is removed: each count is a line at the end of its section.
  2. **A section without data** shows no zeros and no controls: a title, one line and one action (`to learning`, `to typing`), without the cat. A guest with no data at all keeps the `EmptyState` with the `sleep` cat and sees no switch. Loading and a load error take the section's place under the switch.
  3. **The track switch belongs to the answer-time chart** and to nothing else. It stands first in that block's controls and is not drawn while only one track has answers. Mastered kana are two figures, one per track.
  4. **Typing filters**, one row above everything they change: the mode (one select of the modes that exist in the history, the mode of the latest test selected at start, plus "all modes"), the period (7 days, 30 days, all; 30 by default) and "with repeats" (off by default). They change the figures of the selection, the chart and the history. Personal bests, the weak kana table and the count of tests stand lower under "All time" and do not follow the filters.
  5. **The typing chart draws every test as a mark at its time.** The line is the median of the last 10 tests of the selection and the band their middle half (25–75%). The personal best of a mode is a ring, a repeat a hollow mark. With "all modes" there is no line and no band. Fewer than five tests: no time axis and no line, five slots with the tests in order, each with its number and date; no tests: a notice with "show all time".
  6. **Four metrics, one at a time:** KPM, kana accuracy, rejected keys (`rejectedKeystrokes / keystrokes`) and hints (`assisted`). The y axis follows the data: KPM takes the tests' own range and is not forced to zero; accuracy runs up to 100% from 90% or lower; rejected keys and hints start at zero. A chart never has two y scales.
  7. **The answer-time chart** draws one bar per day: the middle half of the day's recall answers with a tick at the median, both from the stored 100 ms bins. With more than 45 days on the axis it draws one bar per week. It has the same period switch and keeps the target line and "lower is better" (design decision 67).
  8. **Activity by day** (`ActivityMap`, new): a calendar grid of 12 to 26 weeks (at most 13 below 640 px), one cell per day. The fill is the number of recall answers of the day on `heat-fur-1…5`; a day without answers is an outlined cell; a square marks a day with a finished typing test; today has an `eye` border.
  9. **Weak kana in typing:** the kana table in its `miss` mode, filled by the share of typing attempts with an error on `heat-miss-1…5`, from the stored `KanaStat`. It covers every saved test.
  10. **No state is colour alone:** a mark's shape, an outline, and the numbers in a tooltip and an `aria-label`. The chart, the activity map and the kana table are one Tab stop each, with the arrow keys inside. Tooltips of the chart and the map have several lines.
  11. **Nothing new is stored and no token is added.** SVG and CSS by hand, no chart library; everything lives in the lazy `/stats` chunk. The dark theme stays the only theme.
- **Justification:**
  - A switch halves the page and makes the scope of every control plain: a filter of the typing section cannot be read as a filter of the learning one. A stronger heading with a dividing line leaves the page three screens long; two columns leave no room for a chart and fall apart on a phone.
  - KPM compares only inside one mode, so the filter picks a mode. Four independent groups (content, script, strictness, length) offer some 300 combinations of which a learner has three to six: most choices would end in an empty selection.
  - A mark per test is honest at every size: one test is one mark, and two hundred show their spread and their direction. One bar per day hides how many tests the day had; four small charts are too small to read a value from. All three were drawn on the same data (`StatsExplorations` in the artifact).
  - The median of the last 10 tests is the window the results screen already uses for its average, so the line and the comparison there agree.
  - A day is the stored unit of learning (answer-time bins per local day), so a bar per day shows what is stored, with its spread, and a day without answers stays empty.
  - The heat maps return because the data is already there: `recallBuckets`, `activeDays`, `endedAt` and `KanaStat`. ADR 058 removed them with the kana speed tests they described.
- **Consequences:**
  - The `docs/design/source/` export is updated: `README.md` ("Stats", "Colour", "Motion"), `decisions.md` (100–112; 68 and 78 marked superseded), `tokens.json` (usage notes only), `bundle.js`, `bundle.css`, `index.d.ts`, the new `ActivityMap` README and the `ResultChart`, `KanaChart`, `Segmented`, `Tooltip`, `EmptyState`, `HistoryTable` and `Notice` READMEs. The previews stay in the artifact.
  - [Metrics and statistics](../spec/metrics.md) describes the screen and the derived figures. The code still shows the P4 screen until the implementation lands; `README.md` and `docs/architecture/baseline-v1.md` change with it.
  - Implementation follows in three pull requests: the data (reading the whole local history for a filter, the series and the day counts), the page structure, then the chart and the maps. The route chunk must stay within the 25 KB lazy route budget and the CSS within 10 KB; neither was measured in the design session.
  - The history lists repeats only with "with repeats" on; before, a repeat was always a row marked with a word.
  - The activity map counts recall answers only: recognition answers are not stored per day. A day that is active without a recall answer is drawn at the first step. Counting every answer would need a new per-day counter.
  - A weak kana table for a period or a mode would need per-kana errors in every `TestResult` (a schema change); it is not part of this decision.
  - "All" is what the device holds: for a signed-in user at most 1,000 results until P5 pages the rest from the server.
  - Open: a light theme (a task for the whole palette); the cells of the activity map are smaller than `touch-min` (a cell only shows a tooltip); the week starts on Monday in both languages.
