# ADR 112: Insights on the Main Thread in Scheduler Callbacks, What Got Stronger From the Last Start, and the Forecast History in Schema 7

- **Status:** In force; the mark of the 300 ms line moved to a ready store by [ADR 113](113-lazy-home-panels-and-the-home-line.md).
- **Context:**
  - The P8 home and the session summary show an outline of the next session, the kana that got stronger since the last session started and a forecast of the next row ([ADR 108](108-p8-design-export.md); [p8-plan.md](../archive/p8-plan.md), D3, D8 and D10). The model computes all three ([ADR 100](100-knowledge-model-fsrs-planner-and-answer-log.md), [ADR 110](110-home-data-from-the-model.md)); nothing in the app calls them yet.
  - The forecast takes about 10 ms in Node for the row the home asks for and about 52 ms in the worst measured state; the fold of 40,000 answers takes 29 ms. At 4× CPU throttling the whole is 100–235 ms, under the 300 ms of [phases.md](../spec/phases.md).
  - The store folds the whole log and keeps no state at the start of a session, so "before" does not exist. The forecast corrects itself by settled forecasts (`history`), and nothing keeps them.
- **Decision:**
  1. **Where.** The outline, what got stronger and the forecast are computed on the main thread, in two callbacks of the injected `Scheduler` (step 1: what got stronger and the outline; step 2: the history and the forecast), never in a render or a key handler. A screen asks with `requestInsights(track)`; `startSession` drops the pending steps, so nothing computes during a session. The signals and the request are in the initial chunk (`insights-state.ts`); the computation and the history are the lazy chunk `insights.ts`, which the store loads together with the model. A request is current while its `learnVersion` and track are; a step that finds it stale publishes nothing. The seeds are fixed (`FORECAST_SEED` 1, `OUTLINE_SEED` 2): the same state gives the same numbers.
  2. **What got stronger** compares `describe` of the state just before the last `start` of the log, of any device, with the current view. The store folds the log up to that `start`, copies the state (`snapshot()`), then applies the rest to the same fold (the cost of the plain fold), and takes a copy before it applies a `start` incrementally. Without a `start` there is no "before" (`first`).
  3. **The forecast history** is the store `forecasts` of IndexedDB (schema 7), keyed `{row}@{local day}`. The first forecast of a row on a day is kept (`raw`, `started`); it settles when the row has no locked kana, with `actual` = the session that introduced the row's last kana minus `started`, and is deleted when that is below 1. The newest 10 settled and 60 open records are kept. The forecast reads the settled ones, which corrects it from the third. The history is held in memory after one read, and dropped when the log is emptied (reset, sign-out, another account, account deletion), so a cleared store does not come back. It is never synced and it goes with the log.
  4. **Schema 7.** `STORES` gains `forecasts`, and a device of schema 6 is reset ([ADR 061](061-schema-reset-before-release.md)); the maintainer syncs first.
- **Justification:** a Worker would copy 1–2 MB of log and fold it again, which costs more than the forecast; callbacks keep Continue and every key handler free, and the measured line holds with a margin. A snapshot at the last start costs nothing beyond the fold the store already does. A history per device means what it corrects: how this learner's log moved against its forecasts.
- **Rejected alternatives:**
  - _A Worker:_ a second copy of the model, a protocol to keep it in step with the log and a larger cost than the work. It returns only if the line of 300 ms is missed.
  - _Folding the log again for "before":_ doubles the cost of a load; the split is free.
  - _Syncing the history:_ it means nothing for another log.
- **Consequences:**
  - [learning.md](../spec/learning.md) describes the forecast and what got stronger; [storage-sync.md](../spec/storage-sync.md) the store and schema 7; [architecture/entities.md](../architecture/entities.md), [model.md](../architecture/model.md) and [sync.md](../architecture/sync.md) the code.
  - No screen shows the insights until PR 4; PR 5 reads the forecast `before` from `insights` when a session starts.
