# ADR 114: The Home Fix: Two Bands Placed by the Window's Height, the Path's Blocks as a Chain of the Kinds of Kana, the Sleeping Cat With Nothing to Do

- **Status:** In force.
- **Context:**
  - After P8 PR 5 the maintainer found too much empty space under the home on a desktop. In the two columns of design decision 140 ([ADR 108](108-p8-design-export.md)) the home's content ended about 480 px from the top of the window in every state: under it about 250 px stayed empty at 1120 × 800, about 350 px at 1440 × 900 and about 650 px at 1440 × 1200.
  - [p8-plan.md](../../p8-plan.md), section 4.3, gave the question to a design session of its own (M5): how the home uses a desktop window in every state, with its content, data and copy fixed. The session ran on 2026-11-25 as [ADR 106](106-design-sessions-in-a-workbench-artifact.md) describes, in one round with three reviews. Its decisions 153–155 are exported here.
  - The maintainer's reviews added two remarks beyond the empty space: the path's blocks did not look like something to press, and the ticks under the blocks and over the rows read as navigation without saying what they meant.
- **Decision:** Design decisions 153–155 in [`decisions.md`](../design/source/decisions.md), exported from the design artifact.
  1. **Two bands on a desktop** (153). From 640px the action (left half) and "since the last session" (right half) stand on top, and the path with the forecast spans `layout-max` under them. The home takes the window between the navigation and the footer and stands at two fifths of its free height, so the free space falls above and below it. Below 640px the screen keeps the order of 140: the action, the path, since the last session.
  2. **The page is built in the order of the screen at its width** (153). The DOM order, and with it `Tab`, follows what the eye sees: on a desktop `continue`, the kana of "since the last session", the path's blocks; below 640px `continue`, the path's blocks, the kana.
  3. **The sleeping cat with nothing to do** (154). The cat `sleep` (96 px) stands over `nothing to review now`, as in the empty states. It is on `/` in this state only; the design's Cat guide changes accordingly.
  4. **The path's blocks are a chain of the kinds of kana** (155). The blocks are a `Segmented` named `blocks of the path` / `блоки пути`, in the order they open; each item shows the block's first kana (`あ`, `が`, `きゃ`; `ア ガ キャ ファ` for katakana) in `jp-test-sm` over the name and the count, with a `→` between blocks. `←` `→` move between them, as on the track switch. The open block's rows lose their ticks: the current row's head is `eye`, and the system's caret stands before the kana that opens next. The kana of the rows are `jp-cell` from 640px.
- **Justification:**
  - The bands use the width and the height of a laptop window: the path gets the whole `layout-max`, its kana are set larger, and the content reaches about 720 px of a 900 px window. Placing the home by the window's height leaves the rest of the space on both sides of it, so on a tall window it does not read as a hole.
  - A DOM order that differs from the screen makes `Tab` jump against the eye. Building the page in the screen's order at each width keeps the phone as it was and `Tab` in step with what is seen; the cost is one media query on the home, which renders again only when the window crosses 640px.
  - With nothing to do the home has no `continue` and no outline: it is the emptiest state. The sleeping cat is the system's sign of an empty state, so she says "nothing now" without new words.
  - The track switch is the page's one familiar sign of "this can be chosen", so the blocks take it. The first kana of a block shows a beginner what "voiced" and "combinations" mean before opening them, and the arrows show the order in which they come. The caret is the system's own sign of "where you are".
- **Rejected alternatives:**
  - _Layout:_ the two columns of 140 placed at two fifths of the window's height (the space only moves); the same with the path larger in the right column (still empty on a laptop, the combinations crowded); the order of the screen kept on a phone with the DOM in the desktop's order (`Tab` against the eye on a phone); the phone in the desktop's order (a long "since the last session" pushes the path down).
  - _Nothing to do:_ the reason's line alone (141).
  - _Path:_ the blocks as the track switch with the count only; the same with a meter of each block; the kana in one line with the name. Ticks over the rows (143) read as tabs.
- **Consequences:**
  - The export changes `README.md` ("Learning home", "Keyboard", "Cat"), `decisions.md` (153–155; 140, 141 and 143 marked as partly superseded) and the guides of `Cat` and `Segmented`. No token, text style or glyph changes: `→` is in the Mono subset (design decision 53), so `tokens.css` and `tokens.test.ts` do not change.
  - P8 PR 7 builds it, with the spec: `learning.md`, "`/` screen" (the layout, the path's blocks, nothing to do) and `docs/architecture/pages.md`. The app's `Segmented` takes options with content beyond a string label.
  - The workbench of the session is deleted after this export is merged.
