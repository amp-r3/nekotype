# ADR 059: Design System Version 10

- **Status:** In force. Amends [ADR 031](031-adopt-the-nekotype-design-system.md). Amended by [ADR 062](062-design-review-of-the-p3-build.md): design decisions 93–96 (the kana details panel, due kana, displayed romaji). Amended by [ADR 065](065-cleaner-cat-and-logo-drawings.md): design decisions 97–98 (cleaner cat and logo drawings, a smiling `content` cat, a reserved `grumpy` cat). Amended by [ADR 076](076-stats-screen-redesign.md): design decisions 100–112 (the `/stats` screen) replace decisions 68 and 78.
- **Context:** [ADR 058](058-learning-first.md) made learning the core of the product, and design system version 9 still drew a typing trainer. Version 10 of the artifact redraws `/` (the kana chart in a `state` mode), the learning session, its summary, typing on `/type`, `/stats` and `/settings`, and carries over the rules the P2 code took after version 9 (ADR 041, 042, 046, 047, 055–057). It is in English, like the rest of the documentation. Its decisions are in `docs/design/source/decisions.md` under "After ADR 058". A few of them change the spec or name the code differently, and the export must not be edited by hand ([Design system](../design/README.md)), so the choice between the design's names and the code's needs recording.
- **Decision:**
  1. **Export.** `docs/design/source/` is the version 10 export. Its structure is that of version 9 with one more component folder, `KanaTask`; previews and fonts stay in the artifact.
  2. **Learning task.** One task of a learning or review session (introduction, recall, recognition) is drawn by a component of its own, `KanaTask`, with a DOM controller `createTaskController` (`setBuffer`, `type`, `reject`, `finish`, `reset`, `setWindowFocus`, `setInputFocus`). It is not a mode of `TestArea`: the stream has many prompts on shifting lines, live indicators and a translation, while a task shows one kana or romaji with a session line, options and a ghost hint, and ends differently (the answer held for 400 ms, a restart when the tab is hidden). The hot-path rules are the same: the controller writes classes, text and transforms only, and the component renders once per task.
  3. **Names.** The design's names are the target. In P3 the code's `Toggle` becomes `Switch` (label and description on the left, the track on the right) and the `ghost` button variant becomes `text`. The other differences the design kept as its rule are fixed in the code in P3 too: no line under `Nav` and under the `Dialog` title, lower-case `en / ru`, and a loading notice that does not blink.
  4. **Key notation.** In hints and documentation, `+` joins keys pressed together (`ctrl + enter`, `⌘ + enter`) and `→` joins keys pressed in turn (`tab → enter`). The word "then" is not used.
  5. **Glyphs.** The design uses five glyphs that no subset has: `·` in JetBrains Mono, `⌘`, `−` (U+2212), the no-break space U+00A0 and the narrow no-break space U+202F. They are added to the subsets of [ADR 034](034-design-tokens-and-font-build.md) in P3, in the fonts listed under "Glyphs to add to subsets" in the design decisions.
  6. **Behaviour the spec takes from the design,** recorded in [Frontend](../spec/frontend.md) and [Learning](../spec/learning.md): a "Show the romaji" button in place of the Esc hint on touch screens; no navigation during a learning session, which is left with "Leave session"; a second action "To the table" on the session summary; separate labels for the learning and typing strictness; the typing config bar folded into one line on a phone.
- **Justification:** The design names what the user sees and is the reference other documents point to; renaming two identifiers in the code is cheaper than keeping a translation table between documents and code. A component of its own for a task keeps `TestArea` free of branches for a screen it does not share, at the cost of a second, small controller. Alternatives rejected:
  - the code's names (`Toggle`, `ghost`): the export cannot be edited by hand, so the mismatch would stay in every document;
  - a `kana` mode of `TestArea` for tasks: one controller would carry two sets of slots and end states.
- **Consequences:** P3 renames `Toggle` and the `ghost` variant in `shared/ui` and at their call sites, rebuilds the subsets with the five glyphs (each font stays within 45 KB), and builds `KanaTask`. The design's open questions are open items in the [spec decisions](../spec/decisions.md) (O7, O8).
